# German Shorthaired Pointer

Breed group: Sporting.

## Overview
A versatile German hunting dog of the 1870s bred to point, retrieve on land and water, and track fur. Lean athletes: 21-32 kg, 53-64 cm. Liver or liver-ticked short coat.

## Temperament
High-energy dogs that bond intensely with their family. Without daily exercise they climb fences and dig craters. Friendly with strangers and other dogs.

## Health
Typical lifespan 12-14 years. Watch for hip dysplasia, bloat, and some cancers. Von Willebrand's disease (a bleeding disorder) appears in some lines.

## Grooming
The easiest wash-and-go coat: weekly rubber-curry brushing, occasional baths. Dry thoroughly after wet work.

## Exercise
2 or more hours of hard running daily for adults. Bred to hunt all day; a walk around the block is a warmup, not exercise. Field training or canicross are ideal.

## Fun facts
Webbed feet make them strong swimmers. The liver nose and noble head are the iconic look of an upland bird dog.
