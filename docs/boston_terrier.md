# Boston Terrier

Breed group: Non-Sporting.

## Overview
The 'American Gentleman': a tuxedo-marked breed developed in Boston in the 1870s from Bulldog and the now-extinct White English Terrier. Weight 4.5-11 kg; height 38-43 cm at the withers.

## Temperament
Gentle, friendly, and eager to please. Sensitive to tone of voice; they respond best to positive methods. Playful well into adulthood.

## Health
Typical lifespan 11-13 years. Brachycephalic: heat intolerance and snoring. Patellar luxation, prominent-eye injuries, and allergies.

## Grooming
Minimal: weekly brushing, facial fold cleaning, regular nail care. White markings can collect tear stains.

## Exercise
30-45 minutes daily; avoid heat. The short face limits exertion in warm weather.

## Fun facts
The AKC's first homegrown American breed, recognized in 1893.
