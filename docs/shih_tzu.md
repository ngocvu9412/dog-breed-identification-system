# Shih Tzu

Breed group: Toy.

## Overview
A toy companion ('little lion dog') bred in Tibetan monasteries and Chinese palaces. Weight 4-8 kg, height about 27 cm. Long flowing double coat in any color; often kept in a short puppy clip.

## Temperament
Affectionate lap dogs bred for centuries as palace companions. Alert and surprisingly sturdy for their size. They want to be near you, not to jog with you.

## Health
Typical lifespan 10-16 years. Brachycephalic: watch breathing in heat. Patellar luxation is common, and the prominent eyes are injury-prone. Dental disease is near-universal in the breed.

## Grooming
Full coat: daily brushing plus monthly professional grooming. Puppy clip: brushing every 2-3 days. Keep the eye area dry and clean to avoid staining and skin issues.

## Exercise
Short daily walks and indoor play; 30 minutes is plenty. They tolerate heat poorly, so walk early in summer.

## Fun facts
DNA studies place the Shih Tzu among the most ancient dog breeds. Tibetan 'little lion dogs' were palace gifts to Chinese emperors.
