# Dachshund

Breed group: Hound.

## Overview
A long-bodied scent hound (German for 'badger dog') bred to hunt badgers in their tunnels. Standards weigh 7-14 kg, miniatures under 5 kg. Three coat types: smooth, longhaired, wirehaired.

## Temperament
Brave to the point of comedy: a small dog bred to fight badgers underground. Stubborn, clever, and clingy with its person. Longhairs are often the calmest variety.

## Health
Typical lifespan 12-16 years, among the longest-lived breeds. The top risk is intervertebral disc disease: roughly a quarter of Dachshunds suffer a disc incident. Weight control and avoiding jumps help.

## Grooming
Smooths need a weekly wipe-down; longhairs brushing several times weekly; wirehairs hand-stripping a few times yearly. All need ear and nail care.

## Exercise
Two 30-minute walks daily plus play. Avoid rough jumping and long stair climbs to protect the spine. Scent work is the breed's original game.

## Fun facts
Waldi, a Dachshund, was the first official Olympic mascot (Munich 1972). Picasso's dog Lump appeared in several of his paintings.
