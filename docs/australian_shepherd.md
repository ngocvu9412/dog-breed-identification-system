# Australian Shepherd

Breed group: Herding.

## Overview
A medium herding dog actually developed in the western United States from European stock brought by Basque shepherds. Weight 18-29 kg, height 46-58 cm. Merle, black, or red, with or without white and tan.

## Temperament
Brilliant, intense, and busy. Bred to control sheep all day; without livestock they may try to herd children and other pets. Reserved with strangers but not shy.

## Health
Typical lifespan 12-15 years. Eye defects (cataracts, collie eye anomaly) are the breed's main concern. The MDR1 gene mutation requires testing before certain drugs. Epilepsy appears in some lines.

## Grooming
Medium-maintenance double coat: brush 1-2 times weekly, more during the spring shed. Never shave; the double coat insulates against heat and cold.

## Exercise
1-2 hours daily of real work: agility, frisbee, or herding trials. Puzzle toys for rainy days.

## Fun facts
Despite the name, the breed has no Australian ancestry. Aussies dominate disc-dog and agility competitions.
