# Labrador Retriever

Breed group: Sporting.

## Overview
A muscular, friendly retriever developed in Newfoundland to haul nets and fish for fishermen. Adults stand 55-62 cm and weigh 25-36 kg. Short dense coat in black, yellow, or chocolate.

## Temperament
Sociable, high-spirited, and food-motivated. Labs mature slowly; adolescent energy can last until age three. Excellent with children, though their enthusiasm can knock over toddlers.

## Health
Typical lifespan 10-14 years. Watch for hip and elbow dysplasia, exercise-induced collapse, obesity, and progressive retinal atrophy. Ear infections are common in swimmers.

## Grooming
Low-maintenance coat: brush weekly with a rubber curry. Heavy shedding twice a year. Bathe every 6-8 weeks and dry ears thoroughly after swimming.

## Exercise
80 minutes to 2 hours daily. Fetch is the signature game; most Labs will retrieve indefinitely. Weight management depends on exercise because the breed is obesity-prone.

## Fun facts
The most popular dog breed in the United States for over 30 consecutive years per AKC registrations. Many guide dogs for the blind are Labs.
