# French Bulldog

Breed group: Non-Sporting.

## Overview
A compact bat-eared companion breed developed in Paris in the 1800s from toy bulldogs. Weight under 14 kg; height about 28-33 cm. Smooth short coat in brindle, fawn, white, or cream.

## Temperament
Affectionate, adaptable, and quietly playful. They want to be with their person constantly and dislike being left alone. Alert enough to announce visitors but rarely noisy.

## Health
Typical lifespan 10-14 years. A brachycephalic (flat-faced) breed: heat intolerance and breathing difficulties are real risks. Most Frenchies cannot swim, and the vast majority of litters are delivered by C-section.

## Grooming
Minimal: weekly brushing and monthly baths. Clean the facial skin folds several times per week to prevent bacterial infections. Routine ear and nail care.

## Exercise
20-30 minutes daily in short walks. Never exercise in heat or humidity; the flat face makes them prone to heatstroke. Keep them lean.

## Fun facts
Overtook the Labrador as the most popular US breed in AKC registrations in 2022-2023. They snore loudly and often 'talk' to their owners.
