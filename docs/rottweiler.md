# Rottweiler

Breed group: Working.

## Overview
A powerful guardian breed descended from Roman cattle dogs that settled in Rottweil, Germany. Males stand 61-69 cm and weigh 43-59 kg. Short black coat with rust markings.

## Temperament
Calm, confident, and courageously loyal. Well-socialized Rotties are gentle and playful at home, aloof with strangers, and slow to accept newcomers.

## Health
Typical lifespan 8-10 years. Prone to hip dysplasia, subaortic stenosis (heart), bloat, and bone cancer. Reputable breeders screen hearts and hips.

## Grooming
Easy coat care: weekly brushing, a few baths per year. Heavy seasonal shedding twice a year.

## Exercise
Two 20-40 minute sessions daily plus mental work. Carting, tracking, and obedience give this working dog a job.

## Fun facts
Rottweilers were butchers' dogs that pulled meat carts to market in Rottweil, hence the German nickname Metzgerhund (butcher's dog).
