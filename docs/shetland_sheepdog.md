# Shetland Sheepdog

Breed group: Herding.

## Overview
The Sheltie, a small herder (5-11 kg, 33-41 cm) from Scotland's Shetland islands, bred to keep sheep out of crofts. It resembles a miniature Collie but is a separate breed. Rough double coat, commonly sable.

## Temperament
Sensitive, smart, and vocal. Devoted to their person and slow to warm to strangers. The bark announces everything.

## Health
Typical lifespan 12-14 years. Collie eye anomaly, dermatomyositis, MDR1 sensitivity, and patellar luxation are the main concerns. Thyroid issues appear with age.

## Grooming
Brush weekly, daily during seasonal sheds; the mane mats if neglected. Never shave the double coat.

## Exercise
60 or more minutes daily plus brain work. Agility is their sport: quick, biddable, and nimble.

## Fun facts
Shelties herd by darting at the heels of sheep. The breed excels at agility competitions worldwide.
