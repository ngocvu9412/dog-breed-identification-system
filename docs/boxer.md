# Boxer

Breed group: Working.

## Overview
A muscular German breed of mastiff descent (Bullenbeisser) once used for hunting large game. Weight 25-32 kg, height 53-63 cm. Fawn or brindle with black mask; about one in five is born white.

## Temperament
The eternal puppy: playful well into old age. Patient with children, alert and wary of strangers. Brachycephalic build limits stamina in heat.

## Health
Typical lifespan 10-12 years. Elevated cancer rates, aortic stenosis, hip dysplasia, and bloat are concerns. White Boxers are often deaf.

## Grooming
Minimal: weekly brushing, occasional baths. White-marked dogs may need sunscreen on pink skin.

## Exercise
60-90 minutes daily; avoid heat. Puppy joints need care until 18 months.

## Fun facts
The name comes from the breed's habit of sparring with its front paws while playing. Boxers were among the first police dogs in Germany.
