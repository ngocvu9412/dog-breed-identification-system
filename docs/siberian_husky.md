# Siberian Husky

Breed group: Working.

## Overview
A medium endurance sled dog (16-27 kg, 51-60 cm) bred by the Chukchi people of Siberia to pull light loads over long distances. Thick double coat and facial mask; eyes brown or blue, or one of each.

## Temperament
Famously friendly and free-spirited: terrible guard dogs, excellent comedians. Pack animals that need company; a lone Husky is a howling escape artist.

## Health
Typical lifespan 12-14 years. Generally hardy. Watch for hip dysplasia, eye issues (cataracts, progressive retinal atrophy), and zinc-responsive dermatosis. Secure fencing is a health-grade necessity.

## Grooming
Heavy seasonal coat blows (clumps of undercoat) 1-2 times yearly: daily brushing then, weekly otherwise. Never shave a double coat.

## Exercise
1-2 hours daily minimum; they were bred to run vast distances pulling sleds. Off-leash recall is famously unreliable. Fenced yards and long lines.

## Fun facts
The 1925 serum run to Nome (the Balto story) was run by Husky teams; Togo covered the longest leg at 145 km. Husky blue eyes are not linked to health problems.
