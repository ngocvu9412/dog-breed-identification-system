# Pomeranian

Breed group: Toy.

## Overview
A tiny spitz breed (1.4-3.2 kg) from Pomerania, downsized from sled dogs in the 1800s; Queen Victoria popularized the small type. Profuse double coat, orange-red most iconic.

## Temperament
Confident, curious, and extroverted. Alert watchdogs that bark readily. They forget their size and will challenge dogs 20 times their mass.

## Health
Typical lifespan 12-16 years. Patellar luxation, collapsing trachea (harnesses over collars), alopecia X, and dental disease.

## Grooming
Brush 2-3 times weekly with a pin brush; the thick undercoat mats. Never shave: the coat may not regrow properly. Daily dental care.

## Exercise
30 minutes daily in short sessions; indoor play works. Watch for overheating in the thick coat.

## Fun facts
Two Pomeranians were among the survivors of the Titanic sinking in 1912, carried off in lifeboats.
