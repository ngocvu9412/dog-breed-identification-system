# Yorkshire Terrier

Breed group: Toy.

## Overview
A tiny terrier (2-3 kg) from Yorkshire, England, bred in the 1800s to catch rats in textile mills. Fine silky steel-blue-and-tan coat that grows continuously and resembles human hair.

## Temperament
Feisty, brave, and bossy in a lap-dog body. Devoted to their person, wary of strangers and other dogs. True terrier spirit.

## Health
Typical lifespan 13-16 years. Luxating patellas, collapsing trachea (use a harness, never a collar), puppy hypoglycemia, and dental disease.

## Grooming
High: a full coat needs daily brushing; most owners keep a puppy cut brushed every 2-3 days. Regular dental care is essential.

## Exercise
Two short walks daily plus indoor play; 30 minutes total is plenty.

## Fun facts
Smoky the Yorkshire Terrier ran telegraph wire through a drainpipe in WWII Luzon and is credited with 12 combat missions, earning 8 battle stars.
