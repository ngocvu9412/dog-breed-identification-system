# Cocker Spaniel

Breed group: Sporting.

## Overview
A compact flushing spaniel from England and Wales, bred to flush woodcock (hence 'cocker'). Weight 12-14.5 kg, height 38-41 cm. Silky medium coat, solid or parti-color. The smallest sporting spaniel.

## Temperament
Merry, gentle, and people-soft. Sensitive to harsh correction; they wilt under yelling. Well-bred Cockers are sweet family dogs.

## Health
Typical lifespan 12-15 years. Ear infections are the signature issue because of the long pendulous ears; weekly cleaning is mandatory. Also hip dysplasia, progressive retinal atrophy, and later-life heart disease.

## Grooming
Significant: professional grooming every 6-8 weeks, brushing 2-3 times weekly. Keep ear canals clean and dry.

## Exercise
60 minutes daily with sniffing time. Scent games in long grass are pure joy for this flushing breed.

## Fun facts
Lady in Disney's Lady and the Tramp is a Cocker Spaniel; the film popularized buff-colored Cockers.
