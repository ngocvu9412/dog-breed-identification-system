# Pug

Breed group: Toy.

## Overview
An ancient Chinese toy breed ('much in little') later favored in Dutch and British courts. Weight 6-8 kg, height 25-28 cm. Smooth coat in fawn or black; curled tail and flat black mask.

## Temperament
Charming clowns bred for centuries to entertain. They thrive on attention and food equally. Minimal exercise needs, maximum companionship needs.

## Health
Typical lifespan 12-15 years. Brachycephalic: snoring and heat intolerance are normal features, heatstroke the danger. Prominent eyes are injury-prone; skin folds need care. Obesity is common.

## Grooming
Easy: weekly brushing, fold cleaning several times per week (keep the nose fold dry), monthly baths, monthly nails.

## Exercise
30-40 minutes daily in two short walks. Avoid heat and humidity entirely. Keep lean.

## Fun facts
A Pug is said to have saved William of Orange in 1572 by barking at an approaching Spanish ambush; the breed became the House of Orange's dog.
