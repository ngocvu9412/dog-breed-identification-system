# Great Dane

Breed group: Working.

## Overview
The 'Apollo of dogs', a giant breed (54-90 kg, 71-81 cm) developed in Germany from mastiff-type boar-hunting dogs. Short coat in fawn, brindle, black, blue, harlequin, or mantle.

## Temperament
Gentle giants that think they are lap dogs. Patient with children and friendly with strangers. Slow to mature: adolescent Danes are clumsy giant puppies.

## Health
Typical lifespan 7-10 years; giant breeds trade size for years. The number-one killer is bloat (gastric dilatation-volvulus); know the emergency signs. Also hip dysplasia and dilated cardiomyopathy.

## Grooming
Minimal: weekly brushing, occasional baths. A giant dog is a lot of surface to wipe after muddy walks.

## Exercise
Moderate: 30-60 minutes daily. No forced running before 18-24 months while joints grow. Soft bedding helps prevent pressure sores.

## Fun facts
Scooby-Doo and Marmaduke are Great Danes. The tallest dog ever recorded (Zeus, 111.8 cm at the shoulder) was a Great Dane.
