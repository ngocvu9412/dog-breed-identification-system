# Poodle (Standard)

Breed group: Non-Sporting.

## Overview
An elegant water retriever originating in Germany over 400 years ago; the fancy clip historically protected joints and organs in cold water. Standards stand over 38 cm and weigh 20-32 kg.

## Temperament
Proud, extremely smart, and people-oriented. Standards are calmer than the smaller varieties. They learn quickly and need daily brain work or they invent mischief.

## Health
Typical lifespan 12-15 years, long for the size. Prone to hip dysplasia, epilepsy, Addison's disease, bloat, and sebaceous adenitis. Standard lines should be screened for Addison's history.

## Grooming
Professional grooming every 4-6 weeks is non-negotiable; the curly coat mats and grows continuously. Brush daily to weekly depending on clip. Ears need regular cleaning.

## Exercise
60-90 minutes daily. They excel at obedience, agility, and dock diving. Swimming is excellent low-impact exercise for the breed.

## Fun facts
Ranked second-smartest dog breed in Stanley Coren's working-intelligence ranking, behind only the Border Collie. Historically famous circus performers.
