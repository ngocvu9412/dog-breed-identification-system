# German Shepherd

Breed group: Herding.

## Overview
A large working breed standardized in Germany in 1899 by Max von Stephanitz for herding and utility work. Males stand 60-65 cm and weigh 30-40 kg. Medium-length double coat, most commonly black and tan.

## Temperament
Loyal, confident, and courageous. Naturally protective without excessive aggression when well socialized. Aloof with strangers until introduced. Bonds deeply to its person or family.

## Health
Typical lifespan 9-13 years. Prone to hip dysplasia, degenerative myelopathy, bloat, and exocrine pancreatic insufficiency. Breeding lines should be screened for hips and elbows.

## Grooming
Brush twice weekly; daily during the two heavy seasonal sheds. A year-round shedder. Bathe only every 2-3 months to preserve coat oils.

## Exercise
2 or more hours daily with a job to do: obedience, tracking, agility, or protection sports. A bored Shepherd invents destructive jobs. Mental stimulation is essential.

## Fun facts
The world's leading police and military dog. The movie star Rin Tin Tin was a German Shepherd found on a WWI battlefield.
