# Chihuahua

Breed group: Toy.

## Overview
The world's smallest breed, named for the Mexican state. Weight 1.5-3 kg, height 15-23 cm. Smooth or long coat in any color. The apple head with a molera (skull gap) is traditional.

## Temperament
Tremendous personality in a tiny frame: loyal to one person, suspicious of everyone else. Big-dog attitude in a 2-kg body. Poor tolerance for rough children.

## Health
Typical lifespan 14-17 years, among the longest-lived breeds. Fragile: top injuries are falls and being stepped on. Dental disease is near-universal in tiny crowded mouths.

## Grooming
Smooths: weekly wipe-down. Longhairs: weekly brushing. Daily dental care is ideal for this breed.

## Exercise
30 minutes daily; indoor play covers most needs. Avoid rough play with large dogs.

## Fun facts
The smallest dog ever recorded, Milly the Chihuahua, stood 9.65 cm tall. The breed descends from the Techichi kept by Toltec civilizations.
