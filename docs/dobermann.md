# Dobermann

Breed group: Working.

## Overview
A German guardian breed created in the 1890s by tax collector Louis Dobermann for protection on his rounds. Sleek athletes: 27-45 kg, 61-72 cm. Black-and-rust most common; also red, blue, fawn.

## Temperament
Loyal, alert, and obedient to their family. Velcro dogs that want constant proximity. Modern breeding has made them far more companionable than their old fierce reputation.

## Health
Typical lifespan 10-12 years. The number-one breed risk is dilated cardiomyopathy; annual heart screening from age 3 is recommended. Also von Willebrand's disease, hip dysplasia, and bloat.

## Grooming
Minimal: weekly brush, occasional wipe-down. One of the lowest-maintenance working coats.

## Exercise
1-2 hours daily with intensity: running, obedience, or protection sports. They crave structured work with their person.

## Fun facts
Dobermanns served as US Marine war dogs in WWII; the first war-dog memorial on Guam honors a Dobermann named Kurt.
