# Golden Retriever

Breed group: Sporting.

## Overview
A friendly, intelligent sporting breed developed in Scotland in the mid-19th century to retrieve shot waterfowl. Adults stand 55-61 cm and weigh 25-34 kg. The double coat is dense and water-repellent, in shades of gold.

## Temperament
Outgoing, eager to please, and gentle. Goldens are famously patient with children and friendly with strangers and other pets. High trainability has made them a top choice for service and therapy work.

## Health
Typical lifespan 10-12 years. Prone to hip and elbow dysplasia, certain cancers (hemangiosarcoma, lymphoma), progressive retinal atrophy, and hypothyroidism. Reputable breeders screen hips, elbows, eyes, and heart.

## Grooming
Brush two to three times per week; daily during spring and autumn shedding seasons. Bathe every 6-8 weeks. Check and clean ears weekly to prevent infections. Trim nails monthly.

## Exercise
Needs 1-2 hours of activity daily: walks, fetch, or swimming. Swimming is a signature activity because the breed was built for water retrieval. Mental exercise such as training games matters as much as physical work.

## Fun facts
Consistently among the most popular dog breeds in the United States. A soft mouth lets a Golden carry an egg without cracking it.
