# Beagle

Breed group: Hound.

## Overview
A sturdy small hound bred in England for hunting rabbits in packs. Two size varieties: under 33 cm and 33-40 cm; weight 9-18 kg. Short coat, typically tricolor.

## Temperament
Merry, curious, and food-obsessed. Pack animals that prefer company of people or other dogs. The nose rules: a Beagle trailing a scent can go deaf to your calls.

## Health
Typical lifespan 12-15 years. Watch for epilepsy, hypothyroidism, intervertebral disc disease, cherry eye, and obesity. Long floppy ears need weekly cleaning.

## Grooming
Low: weekly brushing and ear cleaning, occasional baths. Moderate year-round shedding.

## Exercise
60-90 minutes daily including off-leash runs in secure areas. A fenced yard is strongly recommended because the breed follows its nose. Scent games are the best enrichment.

## Fun facts
Snoopy from the comic strip Peanuts is a Beagle. The breed's distinctive baying voice gave it the old French nickname 'begueule' (open throat).
