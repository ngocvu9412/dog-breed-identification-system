# Border Collie

Breed group: Herding.

## Overview
The world's premier sheep herder, developed in the Anglo-Scottish border country. Medium dog: 14-20 kg, 46-56 cm. The intense stare ('the eye') it uses to control sheep is the breed's signature.

## Temperament
The smartest known dog breed: a problem-solver and workaholic. Not a casual pet; without a job it will disassemble your house. Good with children when raised with them and given enough work, but their herding instinct may nip at running kids.

## Health
Typical lifespan 12-15 years. Collie eye anomaly, hip dysplasia, MDR1 drug sensitivity, and neuronal ceroid lipofuscinosis are the main concerns.

## Grooming
Moderate: brush 2-3 times weekly, more during shedding seasons.

## Exercise
2 or more hours daily PLUS mental work. In its element this breed herds sheep all day. Agility, flyball, or herding trials are ideal outlets.

## Fun facts
Ranked the smartest breed by Stanley Coren. Chaser the Border Collie learned the names of over 1,000 toys and fetched each by name, a milestone in animal-language research.
